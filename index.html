Prior Work Penalty (PWP) Scaling Table

Formula: 2N - 1

Each time an item is modified in an anvil, its internal Anvil Use Count increases by 1. When combining two items, the new item takes max(NA, NB) + 1.

Anvil Uses (N) PWP Level Cost (2N - 1) Base Status Modifiable?
0 0 levels Fresh / Reset ✔ Fully Modifiable
1 1 level 1st Anvil Use ✔ Fully Modifiable
2 3 levels 2nd Anvil Use ✔ Fully Modifiable
3 7 levels 3rd Anvil Use ✔ Fully Modifiable
4 15 levels 4th Anvil Use ⚠️ High Penalty
5 31 levels 5th Anvil Use 🚨 Risk of "Too Expensive!"
6 63 levels 6th Anvil Use ❌ ALWAYS "Too Expensive!" (≥ 40)

💡 How to Reset Prior Work Penalty:

  • Grindstone: Completely resets Anvil Use Count back to 0. Note: Removes all enchantments (except Curses) and grants XP.
  • Crafting Grid Repair: Placing two damaged identical items in a crafting grid resets PWP to 0, but removes all enchantments.
  • Smithing Table: Upgrading Diamond gear to Netherite using a Netherite Upgrade Template preserves existing enchantments and does NOT increase PWP!

Official Minecraft XP Formulas

In Minecraft, XP required per level increases non-linearly. Spending 30 levels from Level 30 costs far fewer XP points than spending 30 levels from Level 50!

Level 0 to 16:

XP to next level = 2 × Level + 7
Total XP = Level2 + 6 × Level

Level 17 to 31:

XP to next level = 5 × Level - 38
Total XP = 2.5 × Level2 - 40.5 × Level + 360

Level 32+:

XP to next level = 9 × Level - 158
Total XP = 4.5 × Level2 - 162.5 × Level + 2220
